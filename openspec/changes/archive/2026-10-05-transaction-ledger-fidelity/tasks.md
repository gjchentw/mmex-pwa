## Findings

- F1 — The draft rules are in a new module, `src/domain/rules/ledger-entry.ts`, not in `ledger.ts` as the tasks said, so the ledger and account rule modules do not import each other. The delta's traceability and design D1 were updated.
- F2 — Two rules were added to the specification delta during implementation: a stored linked transaction is refused by the save operation (desktop opens it in its share or asset dialog), and a status change recomputes a linked position (a void trade leaves the cost book; `investment-tracking` already required it). Also added: empty notes and number are written as the empty string. Design D1 and D5 record them.
